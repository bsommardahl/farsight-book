# Farsight Chapter Quality Pass 3

This is a follow-up mini quality pass on:
- `on-fire.md`
- `availability.md`

after the most recent deepening of:
- `On Fire` section 8
- `Availability` section 5

---

## Executive verdict

Both chapters are now clearly stronger than they were one pass ago.

### `On Fire`
Now feels structurally persuasive from beginning to end.
It is no longer just a credible opening; it is becoming a compelling one.

### `Availability`
Now feels much more coherent through the middle.
It increasingly reads like the lived outcome of the whole book rather than a practical add-on.

---

## 1. `On Fire`

## What improved

### A. The chapter now lands better
Section 8 was the right place to deepen next.

It now does several important things well:
- warns against nostalgia
- reframes awakening as mercy and exposure
- asks follow-up questions that provoke self-examination
- hands the reader toward the diagnosis chapter more convincingly

That gives the chapter a stronger final movement.

### B. The chapter now has a clearer internal progression
The current progression feels good:
- recognition
- interpretation of spiritual intensity
- correction of emotionalism
- conversion as awakening
- fading intensity
- wrong question
- what God is doing
- invitation forward

That is a strong chapter skeleton with real prose in the right places.

## What still feels uneven

### A. Opening and Section 1 still overlap
This is still the biggest editorial issue in the chapter.

Both do the work of recognition. Both mention the same kinds of experiences. Both create a strong beginning, but together they may slightly delay forward movement.

This is now less of a structural problem and more of a later editing problem.

### B. Section density is now better, but not perfectly even
The chapter is much healthier, but some sections are still clearly more scaffolded than others.
Still, this is now normal manuscript unevenness, not conceptual weakness.

## Current verdict on `On Fire`
This chapter is now **good enough to keep as chapter 1 with confidence**.

That is the biggest conclusion from this pass.

The main remaining work is refinement, not rescue.

### Best next move for `On Fire`
A later pass should:
- trim overlap between opening and Section 1
- smooth density between stronger and lighter sections
- possibly compress some scaffolding language once full drafting continues

Not urgent now.

---

## 2. `Availability`

## What improved

### A. Section 5 now does real work
This was the correct next section to deepen.

Why it is better now:
- more explicit correction of platform/ministry-identity thinking
- clearer distinction between heroism and participation
- better connection between surrender and mission
- better language about God already being at work

That improves the chapter's center significantly.

### B. The middle of the chapter is now more coherent
Before, `Availability` had a strong opening and ending but a thinner middle.

Now the middle sections work together much better:
- surrender to the Spirit
- increasing availability over time
- stewardship of life
- joining God in what He is already doing
- church life
- compassion toward the lost

That sequence now feels much more intentional.

### C. The chapter now sounds more like a wake-up call
The additions to sections 3, 5, and 7 have made the tone much more aligned with Byron's clarified aim.

It now speaks more forcefully against:
- protected living
- platform thinking
- shallow church self-centeredness
- irritation toward the lost

That is a gain.

## What still feels uneven

### A. Section 8 is now the thinnest obvious section
“Celebrate every new view” is still good conceptually, but currently lighter than the surrounding sections.

### B. Some scaffold language still remains in the chapter
This is improving, but `Availability` still shows its outline-to-manuscript origins more than some other chapters.

That is not a problem yet. It is just the next editing horizon.

## Current verdict on `Availability`
This chapter now **works as the book’s ending chapter with real weight**.

That is a strong result.

Its remaining issue is not whether it belongs. It is whether every section has reached the same depth.

### Best next move for `Availability`
Deepen **Section 8: Celebrate every new view**

Why:
- it is now the clearest thin spot
- it would improve tonal balance near the end
- it would give the chapter a stronger sense of hope/gratitude without softening the challenge
