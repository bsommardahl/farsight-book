# PROJECT.md

## Title
Farsight

## Type
Book project

## Audience
Christians who need a more eternal, God-centered lens for understanding spiritual life, salvation, sanctification, suffering, purpose, and maturity.

## Working Thesis
The Christian life is not mainly about behaving better in this world. It is about learning to see reality as God sees it and being prepared for eternity in His presence.

## Core Promise
This book helps the reader understand spiritual maturity as renewed sight rather than mere moral performance or fleeting religious intensity.
