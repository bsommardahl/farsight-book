# Farsight Chapter Quality Pass 2

This is a mini quality pass on:
- `on-fire.md`
- `availability.md`

after the latest targeted deepening work.

The purpose is to identify:
- what improved
- what still feels thin
- what should happen next

---

## Executive read

Both chapters are better.

### `On Fire`
Now feels much closer to a real opening chapter instead of an unusually strong outline.

### `Availability`
Now feels more substantial in the middle and more aligned with the wake-up-call tone Byron wants.

If I had to summarize the change simply:

- `On Fire` now has a stronger corrective spine
- `Availability` now has a stronger surrender + mission spine

---

## 1. `On Fire`

## What improved

### A. Section 4 is no longer thin
The conversion-as-awakening section now does real work.

Why it works better now:
- Paul is the right anchor
- the section is clearer that conversion is not mere affiliation
- the chapter now connects awakening, conversion, and sight more cleanly

This significantly helps the book's opening logic.

### B. Section 6 is much sharper
This was one of the chapter's most important weak spots, and it is now much stronger.

What improved:
- stronger wake-up-call language
- clearer critique of emotional Christianity without surrender
- clearer distinction between intensity and transformation
- stronger corrective question at the end

The line of thought is now much more forceful and more aligned with Byron's clarified intent.

### C. The chapter now has a better internal progression
It currently moves more convincingly through:
- recognition
- interpretation
- warning
- correction
- invitation

That is what the chapter needed.

## What still feels weak or uneven

### A. Opening + Section 1 still overlap somewhat
This is still the main editorial drag.

The chapter opens with recognition, then Section 1 also begins with recognition and a human story.
That is not fatal, but it does mean a later pass should decide whether:
- the opening gets slightly compressed, or
- Section 1 gets slightly shortened,
so the chapter reaches new ground faster.

### B. Section 8 remains thinner than the surrounding sections
It is conceptually right, but still more scaffolded than drafted.

That matters because it is the chapter's final interpretive move before the handoff.

## Current verdict on `On Fire`
This chapter is now **credible as chapter 1**.

That is a real milestone.

It is still somewhat uneven, but no longer feels like the weakest structural link in the book.

### Best next move for this chapter
Deepen **Section 8: Awakening is an invitation, not an ending**

That would strengthen the landing and make the handoff into `Nearsighted` more forceful.

---

## 2. `Availability`

## What improved

### A. Section 3 is much better
This section now has a real internal logic.

It no longer just says availability grows. It now shows:
- growth in layers
- God pressing beyond obvious sin
- guardedness being exposed over time
- “personality” and “wisdom” sometimes being revealed as self-protection

That is strong and useful.

### B. Section 7 is much stronger
This section needed more force, and now it has it.

What improved:
- stronger critique of shallow Christianity's attitude toward the lost
- more prophetic clarity
- better emotional and spiritual contrast
- clearer movement from offense to compassion

This section now sounds much more like a wake-up call and less like a generic missions paragraph.

### C. The chapter now feels less like “application”
This is the biggest structural win.

It increasingly reads like:
- the existential outcome of renewed sight
- what a transformed life actually becomes
- the lived answer to the whole book

That is exactly where this chapter needs to go.

## What still feels weak or uneven

### A. Section 5 remains thin
“Join God in what He is already doing” is still more concept than developed prose.

That matters because it is one of the chapter's major practical/theological transitions.

### B. Section 8 remains thin
“Celebrate every new view” is a good idea, but right now it is not carrying much weight.

### C. The chapter still has more scaffold language than some others
This is improving, but it is still not as dense or integrated as `Salvation` or `Sanctification`.

## Current verdict on `Availability`
This chapter now works as a real ending chapter.

It is not fully mature yet, but it no longer feels like a tagged-on practical appendix. It feels like the lived consequence of the whole argument.

That is major progress.

### Best next move for this chapter
Deepen **Section 5: Join God in what He is already doing**

Why this next:
- it would strengthen the move from inward surrender to outward participation
- it would connect the chapter's personal and missional dimensions
- it would reduce the remaining sense that the middle is thinner than the opening and ending
