# Farsight Chapter Source Map

This file maps existing material into the proposed chapter structure so drafting can happen deliberately instead of by rummaging.

---

## 1. On Fire / Awakening

### Existing source files
- `chapters/on-fire.md`
- `graveyard/stories.md`
- `graveyard/meta.md`

### Best material already present
- spiritual highs
- conversion experiences
- boredom after initial zeal
- the question: what is God doing when He seems to be trying to "get through" to us?

### What belongs here from the graveyard
- contrast between wanting fanfare and wanting transformation
- wanting gifts vs wanting the Spirit for change
- testimony candidates that show awakening, disruption, hunger, surrender

### Missing pieces to write from scratch
- a compelling chapter opening
- a bridge from experience to thesis
- a clean statement that awakening is about altered sight, not merely heightened feelings

### Drafting goal
Write a chapter that helps readers recognize themselves before confronting them.

## 2. Nearsighted / The Problem of Spiritual Myopia

### Existing source files
- `chapters/near-sighted.md`
- `graveyard/random.md`
- `graveyard/stories.md`

### Best material already present
From `chapters/near-sighted.md`:
- believers living with a temporal mindset
- death treated as bad even for believers
- people treated as obstacles
- misreading scripture through temporal assumptions
- salvation reduced to a box to check

From `graveyard/random.md`:
- myopic Christianity
- sin as waste of time
- Christian atheism
- spiritual metrics
- abundant life / prosperity critique
- the problem / the solution
- temporal life treated as ultimate reality

From `graveyard/stories.md`:
- behaving well vs surrendering to God
- following Jesus for earthly benefit vs because He leads to eternity
- repenting to avoid losing heaven vs repenting because we crave transformation
- hearing sermons as obligation vs expecting renewal

### Missing pieces to write from scratch
- a stronger opening section
- transitions between symptoms and examples
- clearer distinction between weak believers and merely nominal Christianity
- sharper conclusion that prepares for the human-lenses chapter

### Drafting goal
Write a diagnostic chapter that feels exposing, not sloppy or indiscriminately accusatory.
